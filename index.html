Privacy Policy — ScanLocker

App: ScanLocker • Package: com.scanlocker.app • Developer: ProDev Infotech • Effective: October 27, 2026 • Version 1.1.0
On-Device Document Privacy: ScanLocker's core document scanning, OCR, and storage operate 100% on-device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your document data. Advertising is provided via Google AdMob (Section 6). Data Safety: No document data collected or shared.

1. Overview

ScanLocker is a document scanner app that captures documents, enhances images, creates PDFs, extracts text with OCR, recognizes business cards and ID cards, and protects files with passwords and app lock. Document scanning and file processing run locally on your device. No user accounts, no cloud database of documents, and no telemetry analytics. Advertisements are served via Google AdMob.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect or Share

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share your documents, photos, personal info, contacts, or financial data. Document processing stays 100% on your device. Advertising identifiers may be processed by Google AdMob as described in Section 6.

  • No third-party document sharing: your scans and passwords are never sold, rented, or sent to brokers, advertisers, or analytics providers.
  • No cloud sync: scanned files, names, OCR text, app-lock state, and PDF passwords stay on the device.
  • User-directed sharing only: a file leaves the device only when you explicitly tap Share and choose another app through Android/iOS share sheet.

3. Permissions — On-Device Use Only

Camera

Purpose: capture documents, ID cards, receipts, and business cards for scanning. Used on-device only and never uploaded.

Photo picker / files access

Purpose: let you import a user-selected image for scanning. Modern Android/iOS photo-picker scoping is used; we do not request broad storage access.

Internet

Purpose: ad delivery via Google AdMob, consent management, opening Google Play or support pages, or update checks. Never used to upload or transmit your documents.

Contact saving

Purpose: save extracted business-card details through the system contact screen. We open the platform contact sheet with pre-filled data instead of reading your contacts list.

Note: ScanLocker does not declare ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.

4. Local Storage & Deletion

  • Stored on-device: scanned PDFs/images in app-private storage (`getApplicationDocumentsDirectory()/ScanLocker`), PDF passwords in platform secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences), app-lock and settings in secure local preferences.
  • Encryption: PDF protection uses 256-bit AES PDF encryption. Loss of password means the encrypted PDF cannot be recovered.
  • Delete anytime: delete files from the in-app document library or Android/iOS Settings > Apps > ScanLocker > Storage > Clear Storage / uninstall. All local data, passwords, and settings are removed permanently. There is no server copy because we have no accounts/servers.
  • Retention: data remains until you delete or uninstall. No automatic cloud backups.

5. Children

ScanLocker is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU); the target audience is 13 and older. The app contains only general-audience document scanning tools with an Everyone content rating. Ads are requested with a maximum ad content rating of G.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ScanLocker is supported by advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). When the app requests an ad, Google may collect and process device identifiers (including the Google advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction events (impressions, clicks) for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your documents are never shared: Google and advertisers never receive access to your scanned images, document contents, OCR extracted text, or passwords. Ad requests contain none of that data.

Consent & Privacy Options: Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form is presented before ads are served. You can review or change your ad privacy choices anytime in the app under Settings > Legal > Ad Privacy Settings.
